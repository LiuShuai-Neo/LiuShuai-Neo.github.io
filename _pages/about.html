About me

I work on robot modeling, motion control, and robotic inspection. My experience combines mechanism design, analytical modeling, trajectory optimization, and hands-on implementation — from simulation and embedded control to physical prototypes and experimental validation.

My research at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology is advised by Prof. Molong Duan in the Manufacturing and Aerospace Automation Lab. I have worked on cable-driven parallel robots, mobile robot localization, and additive manufacturing systems.

Career interests: Robotics R&D · Motion control · Autonomous systems engineering
